How this world works 227 ideas live here as beasts
Islands = familiesEach of the 13 islands is one family of ideas on one topic: misalignment, deception, interpretability, philosophy of mind… Islands that share many links sit next to each other, so walking towards an island usually brings you closer to its beasts. Hover an island to see whom it trades with.
Beasts = ideasBody colour is the idea's role in the argument: red failure modes, green solutions, yellow theses, blue lenses, brown critiques, white mechanisms and evidence. Bigger beasts are the core canon.
Lines = relationsClick a beast and its neighbours come to it: what feeds it on the left, what follows from it on the right, rivals above, kin below. Each kind of line moves differently: beads for logic, flowing dashes for cause, a zigzag for debate, dots for kinship, paw prints for history.
Three ways to play Wander: click any beast and read its council. Expedition: get from one beast to the flag by hopping along links; pick a rule (any link, logic, cause, or through time), read a neighbour before stepping, watch warmer / colder. Chronicle: watch the bestiary grow from myths to the LLM era.
